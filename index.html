About Magnolia Recreational Center

Welcome to Magnolia Recreational Center, the ultimate destination for relaxation, fun, and entertainment. Located in the heart of East Aviation, Airport Road, Tamale, we offer a unique combination of luxury and leisure with various services to cater to all ages. Whether you're looking to relax at our VIP lounge, enjoy a delicious meal, or have fun with your family, we've got it all.
